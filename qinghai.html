青海的高海拔、低温、低压和复杂地形，使它天然成为高空探测、无线通信和飞行器环境适应性研究的现场。这里的条件不容易，但它能很快暴露一个系统真正的问题。

Qinghai’s altitude, cold, low pressure and complex terrain make it a natural field context for atmospheric sounding, radio communications and environmental flight testing. The conditions are demanding, but they reveal the real weaknesses of a system quickly.
