SOUNDING CALCULATOR

一个纯前端实时计算工具，用于气球探空方案的参数估算和复核。A front-end real-time calculation tool for estimating and reviewing balloon sounding plans.

METHOD / SCOPE

网页使用 React 与 Tailwind，实现单文件响应式界面。计算链路包含 ISA 对流层与平流层模型、达因公式（WMO 推荐业务标定，二分法求解）、理想气体方程和气体比举力。

输入包含气球克重、实际克重误差、载荷、升速、气体类型、纯度、温度和压力。页面实时估算温度、气压、密度、地面充气体积、实际净举力、内外压差、对流层顶和爆炸高度；SVG 图形显示任务节点，并生成 ASCII 操作台账。

The single-file responsive interface uses React and Tailwind. Its calculation chain combines ISA tropospheric and stratospheric models, the WMO-recommended Dines formula solved by bisection, the ideal gas law and gas buoyancy.

Inputs include balloon mass, mass compensation, payload, ascent rate, gas type, purity, temperature and pressure. The page estimates atmospheric variables, launch volume, net lift, pressure difference, tropopause and burst altitude, visualizes mission nodes with SVG and exports an ASCII operation log.
