沱沱河Tuotuohe

我的探空系统试验地点。The field-test location for my sounding system.

地点与气象站The place & weather station

沱沱河位于青藏高原腹地的长江源区，青藏公路与铁路经此通过。高海拔、低温和稀薄空气，使这里与低海拔测试环境很不一样。Tuotuohe lies in the Yangtze headwater region of the Tibetan Plateau, on the Qinghai–Tibet road and railway routes. High elevation, cold and thin air make it very different from low-altitude test environments.

沱沱河气象站海拔约 4,533 米，是全国第二高的探空站。该表述来自青海省气象局公开材料；不同公开报道的海拔四舍五入值略有差异。Tuotuohe Meteorological Station is about 4,533 m above sea level and is described in Qinghai Meteorological Bureau material as the second-highest sounding station in China. Public reports round the elevation slightly differently.

我的现场记录My field record

2026 年 7 月 19–22 日，我在沱沱河与三江源开展自主控制可回收探空系统的低空和高空试验。试验内容、审批沟通及技术文件归入项目页。On 19–22 July 2026, I conducted low- and high-altitude trials of the autonomous recoverable sounding system in the Tuotuohe and Sanjiangyuan region. Test details, coordination and technical files belong to the project page.
