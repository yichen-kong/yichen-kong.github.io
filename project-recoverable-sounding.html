RECOVERABLE SOUNDING

把一次性探空仪改造成可回收、可维护、可迭代的飞行系统。A recoverable, maintainable and iterative flight system for atmospheric sounding.

系统概述System overview

系统由通信、飞行器（包含探空装置）、地面站和业务计算终端组成。装置由标准 750 g 高空气球搭载升空，达到约 30,000 m 后与气球分离，以变斜翼无动力滑翔实现远程精确回收。研究关注的不只是飞行器本身，也包括通信、制造、控制与业务使用的完整闭环。

The system combines communications, a flight vehicle with the sounding payload, a ground station and an operational terminal. A standard 750 g balloon lifts the system to approximately 30,000 m, where the vehicle separates and performs a long-range unpowered glide for recovery. The work treats aircraft, communication, manufacturing, control and operations as one loop.

工程组成Engineering layers

气动布局Aerodynamics

设计“变斜翼融合弹翼”布局，通过实时调整机翼斜角提高高空抗风性能与远距离飞行效率。A morphing swept-wing and missile-fin configuration improves high-altitude wind resistance and range.

仿真与建模Simulation

使用 SolidWorks、XFlow、XFLR5、ANSYS-Fluent / STAR-CCM+进行参数化建模和 CFD 迭代。Parametric modeling and CFD iterations using SolidWorks, XFlow, XFLR5 and ANSYS-Fluent / STAR-CCM+.

飞控与返航Flight control

采用 MBD、自整定 PID 与总能量管理策略，处理气球分离瞬间的非线性扰动。MBD, auto-tuning PID and total-energy control address nonlinear disturbances after balloon separation.

无线通信RF communications

自研 UHF 链路，结合 LoRa CSS 与 12 单元高增益八木天线，实现低功率双向传输。A custom UHF link combines LoRa CSS with a 12-element high-gain Yagi antenna for low-power bidirectional transmission.

硬件与制造Hardware & manufacturing

设计六层高频 PCB，使用 PMI 泡沫、HT-PLA-GF 和 PA11，在轻量化与结构强度之间取平衡。A six-layer high-frequency PCB and PMI foam, HT-PLA-GF and PA11 parts balance mass and structural strength.

地面站与终端Ground station & terminal

围绕 MAVLink 链路和 ISA 模型开发探空地面站与业务计算终端。A MAVLink ground station and operational terminal are built around ISA atmospheric models.
