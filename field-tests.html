INTO THE FIELD

沱沱河国家高空气象观测站与三江源试验记录。Field testing at Tuotuo River National High-Altitude Meteorological Station and Sanjiangyuan.

环境与方法Environment & method

试验地点海拔约 4,534.5 m，具有寒冷、低压和强湍流等环境特征。试验分别覆盖无人机带载低空测试和约 30,000 m 高空测试，用于验证装置在恶劣环境下的性能与稳定性。

试验前，已就空域和任务安排联系中国科学院空天信息创新研究院、中国科学院大气物理研究所、格尔木市气象局、西宁市国家高空气象观测站、沱沱河国家高空气象观测站等单位。试验过程以安全、合规和科研诚信为前提。

The site is approximately 4,534.5 m above sea level, with cold temperatures, low pressure and strong turbulence. Testing covered drone-assisted low-altitude operations and a planned approximately 30,000 m balloon test to validate performance and stability in harsh conditions.

Before testing, airspace and mission planning were discussed with relevant CAS institutes and meteorological stations. Safety, compliance and research integrity remained the governing principles.
