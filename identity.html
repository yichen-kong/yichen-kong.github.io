名称Names

研驰宸迹研驰宸迹

这是我在 B 站、知乎、嘉立创开源硬件平台等国内网站使用的昵称。“研”代表科研科创，“驰”代表我对汽车、赛车和骑行的热爱，“宸”取自我的名字。与“宸迹”组合，表达追寻理想、走出自己人生轨迹的愿望。This is the name I use on Chinese platforms including Bilibili, Zhihu and the JLCPCB open-source hardware community. “研” refers to research, “驰” to my interest in cars, motorsport and cycling, and “宸” comes from my name. Together, the name expresses my wish to follow my interests and find my own path.

Chen LapLabChen LapLab

在 YouTube、GitHub 等国际网站，我使用 Chen LapLab、Chen_LapLab 或 LapLab。“Chen”是“宸”的拼音；“Lap”是一圈，来自赛车运动；“Lab”是 Laboratory 的缩写。On international platforms such as YouTube and GitHub, I use Chen LapLab, Chen_LapLab or LapLab. Chen is the pinyin of 宸, Lap is the unit of a racing circuit, and Lab is short for laboratory.

标志The mark

“Lap”与“Lab”的外形接近。左边的 L 借用了内燃机转速表的分段显示方式，颜色由低转速向高转速变化；右边分别是“ap”和“ab”。右下角为了视觉平衡，没有严格保持对称。Lap and Lab have similar letterforms. The L on the left borrows the segmented display of an engine tachometer, with colors progressing from low to high revs. The right-hand forms are “ap” and “ab”; the lower-right corner departs from strict symmetry for visual balance.

灵感来自数字分段式和液晶光条式仪表：从 Daytona、Corvette C4、Soarer、S2000，到 Reventón、RS6/7、720S、F80 和赛车方向盘。它们清晰、快速的转速显示，是我喜欢的设计。下方两张拼图来自我提供的说明文档，作为设计参考。The inspiration is segmented digital and LCD-bar instrumentation: Daytona, Corvette C4, Soarer and S2000, followed by Reventón, RS6/7, 720S, F80 and racing steering wheels. I like their quick, readable RPM displays. The following collages are references included in my supplied design notes.

这些参考图片只用于说明标志的设计来源。These reference images illustrate the origins of the mark.
